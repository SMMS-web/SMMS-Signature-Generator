How to add it to Gmail

  1. Fill in your information on the left, then click Copy Signature.
  2. Open Gmail on your computer.
  3. Click the Settings gear in the top-right corner, then click See all settings.
  4. On the General tab, scroll down to the Signature section.
  5. Click Create new, give the signature a name, and click Create (or select an existing signature).
  6. Click inside the signature editor and paste with Ctrl+V on Windows or ⌘+V on Mac.
  7. Scroll to the bottom of the settings page and click Save Changes.

If the icons or logo do not appear correctly, try copying the signature again from the generator and pasting it into Gmail.
