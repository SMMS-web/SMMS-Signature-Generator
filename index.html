Common Questions

Should I set this up on my computer or my phone?
On a computer. The full signature, with the school logo, is best set up on a computer. Complete the steps below once on a computer to use the signature when sending email from that computer.

Will this signature show up on emails I send from my iPhone?
Not automatically. The Mail app on your iPhone keeps its own signature, separate from the one above, and it supports plain text in its signature settings. To set a simple one, open the iPhone Settings app, tap Apps, then Mail, then Signature. On older iPhones, go to Settings, then Mail, then Signature. Type a few lines such as your name, title, and St. Martin de Porres Marianist School.

I clicked Copy Signature and nothing seemed to happen.
Go to Gmail and try pasting. If your browser asked for permission, make sure you clicked Allow. If copying still fails, click open this page in its own tab and try again.

When I pasted, the logo and colors were gone.
This can happen when the signature is copied on a phone or pasted into the wrong box. On a computer, open Gmail Settings, then See all settings, then General, then Signature. Paste using Ctrl+V on Windows or Command+V on a Mac.

My title, extension, or email changed. Do I have to redo it?
Yes, but it only takes a minute. Return to this page, update your information, click Copy Signature, and paste it over your old signature in Gmail. Click Save Changes when finished.

It still is not showing up on my emails.
In Gmail signature settings, check Signature defaults and choose your SMMS signature for new emails and replies/forwards. Then scroll to the bottom of the settings page and click Save Changes.

Preview

Preview updates as you type. Use “Copy Signature” from the published HTTPS site, then paste into Gmail. The logo and icons use public image links to keep the signature small.

How to add your Signature to Gmail

  1. Fill in your information on the left, then click Copy Signature.
  2. Open Gmail and click the Settings gear icon in the top right, then See all settings.
  3. Under General, scroll to Signature.
  4. Click Create new, give it a name (for example, “SMMS”), and paste into the signature box using Ctrl+V or Cmd+V.
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose your new signature for new emails and replies/forwards.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save Changes.
